Our Schools Now: The Tax-Increase Campaign an "Anti-Tax-Increase" PAC's Parent Owns

A $2 million 2018 ballot campaign to raise taxes for Utah schools — run by the identical four officers as Education First Utah's Executive Committee, owned by the same parent nonprofit, and formally declared as an affiliated organization on its own state filing.

Created as a PIC 11/9/2016 $2,029,434.54 raised, 2018 Also known as "Our Kids 1st" & "Utah Students for Question 1"

New here? Our Schools Now was a real, high-profile 2018 Utah ballot campaign to raise taxes and increase school funding statewide. It's registered with the state as a Political Issues Committee (PIC) — a different category than a candidate-focused PAC, which is why it doesn't turn up in a standard PAC search. What makes it relevant to this project: its own state filing names "Education First" as its formally affiliated organization, and its four governing officers are the exact same four people who run Education First Utah's Executive Committee.

1. What Was This Organization?

Our Schools Now's own Articles of Incorporation, filed 10/12/2016, state its purpose in plain language: "to pass a citizen's ballot initiative to increase funding for Utah schools." It was organized under IRC 501(c)(4). Its PIC registration with the Utah Lieutenant Governor's office — created 11/9/2016 — lists two additional names it operated under: "Our Kids 1st" and "Utah Students for Question 1," the latter naming the actual 2018 ballot measure (Question 1) the campaign was built around.

A separate DBA, "Our Schools Now PAC" (Utah entity #11248246-0151), was filed 4/8/2019 — active to this day, last renewed 2022. Its own principal-information filing names its owner directly: "Education First, A Utah Nonprofit Corporation" — the exact same parent entity (#10572869-0140, same "Corportion" misspelling) that owns the Education First Utah DBA covered in this project's companion case file.

2. The Education First Affiliation

This isn't an inferred connection. Our Schools Now's own PIC registration with the state has a field for "Organizations Affiliated with the PIC" — and it names "Education First," at the identical phone number (801) 693-2803, PO Box 10, Centerville, UT address used by the PIC's own Secretary/Treasurer.

5. Worth Asking

  • A single parent nonprofit owns two differently-branded PACs — one that says it doesn't advocate tax increases, one that spent $2 million trying to pass one. Is that two organizations with genuinely different positions, or one operational team keeping both messaging options available depending on the moment?
  • Management & Training Corporation, a private prison company, gave over $100,000 directly to a public school funding campaign — while its own general counsel, treasury director, and CEO run the parallel PAC that avoids taking a tax position at all. What does a private corrections contractor gain from either position on school funding?
