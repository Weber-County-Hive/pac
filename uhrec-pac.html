UHREC: $4.29 Million, One Signature Authority

The Utah House Republican Election Committee has moved over $4.29 million combined since 2018 — nearly double what an earlier partial pull found. Three candidates already documented on this project's other pages received confirmed five-figure totals, and its Treasurer holds the identical role at two other PACs paying his own company.

Primary Officers: Mike Schultz, Casey Snider, Candice Pierucci CFO/Treasurer: Kyle Palmer — sole signature authority $2,265,886.77 in / $2,030,243.42 out, 2018-2026

New here? UHREC is the Utah House Republican Election Committee — the caucus-wide leadership PAC that funds Republican House candidates statewide, distinct from Speaker Schultz's own personal leadership PAC (see that separate case file) though the two run coordinated fundraising and share the same Treasurer.

1. What Is This PAC?

UHREC's own registration lists Mike Schultz (Speaker) and Casey Snider (Majority Leader) as Primary Officers, Candice Pierucci (Majority Whip) as an Officer, and Kyle Palmer as Treasurer/CFO/Director with sole signature authority over disbursements — the same Palmer who holds the identical role at House Speaker Leadership PAC and NUPAC, and who personally owns Irish Elk LLC, a vendor all three PACs pay.

5. The Coombs Connection

UHREC paid Chris Coombs $128,838 across the years on file. Utah's corporate registry shows Coombs is a Director of "Friends of Spencer Cox" — Gov. Cox's own campaign committee — registered at 136 S Main St Ste A200, Salt Lake City. That is the exact same address as Election Hive LLC, Matthew Lusty's political consulting firm, itself paid $84,303.76 by UHREC across the years on file (more than three times an earlier partial estimate of $24,196.56).

6. Worth Asking

  • UHREC, House Speaker Leadership PAC, and NUPAC all funnel large sums to the identical Kyle Palmer-controlled vendor, Irish Elk LLC. At what combined scale does routine multi-client PAC administration become something else worth naming differently?
  • Right Insight received $125,727.42 as "the prior UHREC vendor before Irish Elk took over." What changed in that transition, and did it affect what services the PAC actually received for the money?
  • "Responsive Gov Action" gave $1,000 this period — it describes itself as the political arm of a nonpartisan election-administration nonprofit. Does a nonpartisan-mission organization giving to a partisan state leadership committee complicate that "nonpartisan" framing, even at a small dollar amount?
