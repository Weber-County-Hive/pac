The Merger That Built the Firm

Lincoln Hill Partners exists in its current form because of a single event: an October 4, 2021 merger between two pre-existing firms, Lincoln Hill Partners and Utah Policy Advocates. The firm's own press release from that day names four principals directly — not four people starting a firm from scratch together, but two established operators combining their books of business under one name.

Confirmed by the firm's own release: "Lincoln Hill Partners and Utah Policy Advocates" announced "a merger of their respective firms," describing the result as "the largest of its kind in Utah." All four principals below are quoted directly in that release.

The Four Principals

Owner, Legislative Insight Consulting LLC

Lincoln Shurtz

Registered lobbyist since 2002 (Legislative Insight Consulting) and separately Business Owner of Lincoln-Hill Partners since May 2021. Former Army tank commander; Director of Government Relations for the Utah League of Cities and Towns for 12 years before founding his own firm.

  • Helped create MIDA and the Falcon Hill Aerospace Research Park — currently an active MIDA lobbyist (1/20/2026–present)
  • 100+ clients since 2013: Northrop Grumman, General Motors, Oracle, Aetna, Molina, Intermountain Healthcare
  • Client list also includes data-center operators (Aligned Data Centers, Vast Data Centers), Flock Safety, T-Mobile, and civil-liberties groups (ACLU of Utah, Equality Utah, Human Rights Campaign)
  • Runs his own individual PAC and co-founded both Lincoln Hill PAC and Lincoln Hill Partners PAC
Registered Lobbyist, Campaign Manager

Casey Hill

Registered lobbyist since 1/18/2013, same address as Shurtz (68 E 300 N, Salt Lake City). Former SVP at EnergySolutions (2012–2019) — the company became his continuous lobbying client the following year and remains one today.

  • Closely tied to Election Hive LLC, the campaign-management firm processing money for Stuart Adams, Spencer Cox, and Derek Brown
  • Controls Gathering Inc., a signature-gathering company that has taken in $150,000+ (2024–2025)
  • 80+ lobbying clients: Qualtrics, Salesforce, Netflix, Snap, Roblox, Lenovo, Ivory Homes, plus a wide municipal/county government client list
  • Co-founder, Lincoln Hill Partners/PAC
  • Also runs his own corporation, Red Hill Strategic Inc. (filed under his full name, Joseph Casey Hill, with his wife Kysha Hill as a Director) — see the Red Hill naming section below
Utah Attorney General

Derek Brown

Practiced law at Sidley & Austin LLP in Washington, D.C. before the merger; the firm's release frames his role as legal/regulatory counsel. He co-founded Lincoln Hill PAC on 4/27/2022 — and remained listed as a Director/Officer even after being elected Attorney General.

  • Controls Integrity Strategies LLC, which donated $10,000 to Lincoln Hill PAC using a dissolved Illinois shell entity's address
  • Documented 2024–2025 total received from this network: $294,000+, including $114,000+ from Election Hive alone
  • As Attorney General, he holds the state's chief law enforcement office
Former Managing Partner, Utah Policy Advocates

Chase Everton

Ran Utah Policy Advocates as Managing Partner for four years (Aug 2017–Oct 2021) before it merged into Lincoln Hill Partners — he brought an existing firm and client book into the merger, not just himself. Earlier career: Utah Republican Party Political Director, then Government Relations at 1-800 Contacts.

  • Lobbying clients include Meta Platforms (since 2017), Utah Jazz, Silicon Slopes Commons, Kane Creek Preservation and Development, Flock, and Qualtrics
  • Worked alongside Derek Brown at Michael Best Strategies' Salt Lake City office years before the 2021 merger — an existing professional relationship, not a new one formed by it
  • His own lobbying client, 1-800 Contacts, directly followed his employment there — he began lobbying for a former/concurrent employer

Scott Ryan Smith — the $80,000 Match

S. Ryan Smith — Qualtrics co-founder, and owner of the Utah Jazz and Utah Mammoth — personally gave Lincoln Hill PAC $80,000. That gift landed 12 days after an identical $80,000 donation from Fides LLC, an entity solely owned and managed by Lincoln Shurtz. Qualtrics became a registered Casey Hill lobbying client the following January.

Fides LLC's own Certificate of Organization states its purpose as: "Political advisory and consulting." Shurtz is his own registered agent, at the same Holladay address tied to its donations.

Two things from the full transaction-level export

Maizee Thompson — a Lincoln Hill Partners Associate, listed on the firm's own site — personally received $5,000 from Lincoln Hill PAC. Lincoln Hill PAC also sent smaller sums to Democratic recipients including then-House Minority Leader Brian King, Senate Minority Leader Luz Escamilla, the House Minority PAC, and Utah's Senate Democratic caucus PAC.

The vendors

Lux Events Inc ($18,007.76 across 3 payments) is LUX Catering & Events, a Salt Lake City catering company founded in 1995.

McCauley & Associates ($8,825 across 7 payments) is the accounting firm of Mike McCauley, who is Treasurer of Utah First PAC — Sen. John Curtis's federal leadership PAC — across multiple election cycles, and was formerly Treasurer of Rep. Burgess Owens' campaign committee ("Burgess 4 Utah"), which drew an FEC complaint (MUR 8066, closed in 2023, no violation found).

Chad Westover — CEO of University of Utah Health Plans — received $5,000 personally. He ran and lost in the 2024 Republican primary for Utah House District 42.

The Round Trip: Lincoln Hill Partners PAC and Lincoln Fillmore

Lincoln Hill Partners PAC funds Fillmore's campaign each cycle — for example, $1,000 on 11/18/2024. The following year, Fillmore's own campaign donated back to Lincoln Hill PAC: $1,000 (7/5/2023) plus $500 (12/27/2023), $1,500 total. Several other individual recipients funded by Lincoln Hill Partners PAC in 2022–2023 (Balderree, Weiler, Harper, Teuscher, Lee) later donated to Fillmore's campaign in 2024 — roughly $4,250 total.

Dan Hemmert

HEMMCO LLC, Dan Hemmert's own lobbying entity, gave Lincoln Hill PAC $5,000 in May 2024. Lincoln Hill Partners itself was a paying HEMMCO client (2/1/2023–8/3/2026).
